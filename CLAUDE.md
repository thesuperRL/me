# Rules for This Repo

## End of Every Interview & Write Loop

At the end of every session where a prompt was discussed and an essay was written or refined, you MUST do the following before closing out:

1. **Save the essay** to `Writtens/<Company>.md` with the prompt and final submitted version.

2. **Update or create the experience file** in `Experiences/` for any role or project discussed. Add new themes, specific details, or story angles that surfaced during the conversation.

3. **Update `Writing_Preferences.md`** with any new preferences, phrasings to avoid, structural choices, or voice notes that came up during the session.

Do not skip these steps even if the session felt minor. Small preferences compound into a useful record over time.
