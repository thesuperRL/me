# Silvestria — Co-Founder and Designer

**Period:** Sep 2023 – Jun 2025 (1 year 10 months)
**Type:** Non-profit LLC

---

## Role

Co-founded a non-profit LLC that designed, built, marketed, and sold custom laser-cut accessories for Bridge card game players at affordable prices. Donated all profits to Bridge4Youth, a non-profit that teaches Bridge to disadvantaged youth.

---

## What Was Done

- Designed and iterated on hardware components through rigorous testing across multiple versions to achieve a secure, convenient design
- Conducted user research to discover and implement product changes
- Collaborated with the American Contract Bridge League to design and market bridge accessories to players
- Donated all proceeds to Bridge4Youth charity

---

## Notes

Non-profit with a genuine community mission. Good for demonstrating entrepreneurial initiative, design iteration, and charitable commitment. Small footprint but clean character signal.
