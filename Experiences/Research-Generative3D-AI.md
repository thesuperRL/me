# Independent Research — Generative 3D AI

**Period:** Mar 2024 – Jun 2024 (4 months, Junior Spring independent study at EPS)
**Output:** Literature review paper

---

## What Was Done

Wrote an (at time of writing) unprecedented literature review on Generative AI in 3D applications, compiling valuable research in non-prohibitive text.

- Examined ~40 research papers over three months
- Investigated groundbreaking concepts including NeRFs (Neural Radiance Fields), which transform geometric intuition into learnable representations
- Traced "chains of research" across different applications and methods
- Identified that the field lacked a comprehensive literature review — the paper filled that gap
- Explored ethical implications of 3D generative AI alongside technical findings
- Presented to Microsoft's 3D AI researchers and received generous praise

---

## Origins

Combined longstanding curiosity about AI with interest in 3D design. Self-initiated: identified a gap in the literature and pursued it as an independent study. Part of a broader interest in research as a method of discovery rather than just implementation.

---

## Connection to Later Work

Directly preceded the Google XDTK internship the following summer. Also informed later interest in how generative AI deepfakes could be used to fool biometric scanners — a thread that connects to CyLab security research.

---

## Notes

Strong story for intellectual curiosity and self-directed research at a young age. The gap-identification and systematic review approach is relevant for research-focused applications.
