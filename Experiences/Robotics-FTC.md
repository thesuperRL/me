# FIRST Robotics (FTC) — Eastside Preparatory School

**Period:** Oct 2021 – Mar 2025 (3 years 6 months)
**Roles:** Member (9th–10th) → Head of CAD Design (11th) → Varsity Captain (12th)

---

## Role

Led an FTC team to build a robot from scratch using mostly custom parts. Achieved a world record and school record in competition level progression, reaching global top 3% of FTC teams worldwide and the first state appearance in school history (2024–25 season).

---

## Responsibilities by Year

**Captain (Senior, 2024–25):**
- Led team strategy and robot planning discussions
- Navigated disputes, work distributions, and time conflicts to maintain progress and a cooperative environment
- Shifted from PF Debate to Congressional Debate to free time for Robotics duties

**CAD Lead (Junior, 2023–24):**
- Designed robot and custom parts in CAD
- Led efforts to integrate strategies and creations of both Software and Hardware teams

**Member (Freshman/Sophomore):**
- First experience coordinating in a large team
- Learned programming and design fundamentals from upperclassmen

---

## Outreach

Regularly conducted outreach to Junior Varsity Robotics and First Lego League teams, teaching programming, design philosophy, and CAD design. Resulted in the creation of the JV Robotics CAD team.
