# Independent Project — Gesture-Controlled Drone

**Period:** Dec 2024 – Mar 2025 (4 months, Senior Winter impact project at EPS)
**Status:** Completed (pending approval at time of college app)

---

## What Was Done

- Programmed a gesture-controlled drone to increase ease of controls learning and allow for longer-distance use
- Investigated gesture recognition methods to improve accuracy
- Employed simulators to demo the project in a safer environment
- Attached add-ons using soldering and other manufacturing techniques to give the drone GPS and gyro access

---

## Goal

Investigate gesture recognition AI's capabilities in search and rescue and other applications.

---

## Notes

Combines hardware (soldering, physical add-ons) with software (gesture recognition, simulators). Shows range beyond pure software development. Small project but demonstrates initiative and hands-on engineering instinct.
