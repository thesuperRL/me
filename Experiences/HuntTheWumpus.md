# Microsoft Hunt the Wumpus — Game Developer

**Stack:** C#, Unity, Blender
**Period:** Nov 2021 – Jun 2024 (2 years 8 months)
**Competition:** Local Seattle game design competition, up to 30 teams from multiple schools. Central theme: hunting a beast in a cave system.

---

## Results

- 2021–22: 1st Place
- 2023–24: 3rd Place + Audience's Choice Award
- (2022–23 season canceled due to COVID)

---

## What Was Built

**2021–22:** 2D top-down roguelike shooter. Programmed enemy attacks and aiming/shooting controls. Created most assets and effects using Unity Editor and Blender.

**2023–24:** 3D first-person roguelike ability shooter. Only 3D game featured in the competition. Lead programmer and lead 3D designer.
GitHub: https://github.com/angadjosan/Copal

## Team Role

- Introduced Microsoft Planner to record tasks and due dates

Consistent results across two competition cycles. Good for showing 3D design skills, game development range, and team coordination. The audience award in 2024 signals accessible, polished work beyond just technical merit.
