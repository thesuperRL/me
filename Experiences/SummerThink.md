# SummerThink — Founder, Manager, and Tutor

**Period:** Jun 2024 – Sep 2024 (4 months)
**Type:** Free online tutoring camp

---

## Role

Founded a free tutoring program teaching four subjects (CAD, Precalculus, Python, Data Science) to middle and high school students who may not have access to similar resources.

---

## What Was Done

- Scouted and recruited the strongest students in each subject as volunteer tutors
- Designated a course lead for each subject to organize syllabus creation
- Personally taught 16 sessions across CAD, Precalculus, and Python
- Served as CAD course lead: created curriculum and organized team materials
- Set manageable deadlines for each milestone and class session
- Scheduled regular individual meetings with each volunteer to monitor progress and attend to their feelings
- Published personal schedules and teaching materials to lead by example
- Created and managed Google Classrooms and Teams Meetings
- Troubleshot all technical issues live during sessions
- 9 total tutors, 4 subjects, 3 weeks

---

## Themes

From the Common App personal essay: the four sides of personality (sensitive nature, analytic skill, competitive desire, collaborative spirit) all came together here. First experience scaling teaching beyond EPS to a broader audience. Strong leadership/compassion story: running a volunteer team with care for both output quality and individual feelings.
