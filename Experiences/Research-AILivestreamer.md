# Impact Project — AI Livestreamer (Senior Spring)

**Period:** Mar 2025 – Jun 2025 (4 months, Senior Spring impact project at EPS)
**Team:** 3 seniors

---

## What Was Done

- Created an AI that fully controls and mimics a livestreamer through compartmentalized gameplay, text content generation, and text-to-voice AI models
- Investigated and recommended ethical guidelines for the use of realistic generative AI technology in entertainment, especially livestreaming
- Presented to over 400 students and faculty in an informative yet humorous way

---

## Notes

Final high school project. Combines technical AI work with ethical inquiry — consistent with the broader interest in AI at the intersection of security, privacy, and human behavior. The public presentation to 400+ people is worth noting for communication skills.
