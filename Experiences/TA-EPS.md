# Eastside Preparatory School — Student Teaching Assistant

**Period:** Sep 2023 – Jun 2025 (1 year 10 months)
**Subjects:** Calculus (2 trimesters), Data Science 1 (1 trimester), Physics (3 trimesters)

---

## Role

First student TA in EPS history. Initiated independently — no formal TA program existed. Approached math teacher directly, then the Head of Upper School, who approved it. Scheduled senior year classes around TA commitments.

---

## What Was Done

- Assisted teachers in lectures and work time using programming, math, and physics expertise
- Guided students with questions to let them resolve their own confusion and build intuition, rather than giving direct answers
- Lectured about concepts in adaptable, innovative ways tailored to each student
- Helped teachers prepare presentations and demonstrations; transcribed useful formulae during them
- Led the class through activities when teachers were absent
- Taught other debaters skills that took me to nationals, hoping they would surpass me
- Taught CAD to Robotics members as one of EPS's best CAD designers
- Launched CAD lessons in SummerThink and recorded them for students after graduation

---

## Origin

Inspired by the Robotics coach (also programming teacher), who scheduled individual post-midterm conferences for personal reflection. While others saw extra work, recognized her devotion to teaching. That inspired the pursuit of TA work.

---

## Themes

Teaching as the most important job: passing torches of knowledge. Strong compassion/leadership angle. The "guided discovery" approach (questions over answers) is a consistent teaching philosophy worth naming explicitly in essays.
