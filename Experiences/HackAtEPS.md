# Hack@EPS / EPSchedule — Club Leader and Head Developer

**Period:** Aug 2021 – Jun 2025 (3 years 11 months)
**Organization:** Eastside Preparatory School + Hack Club International

---

## Role

Co-founded Hack@EPS (a rebrand of EPSchedule Club in collaboration with the international Hack Club Organization) to cultivate programming interest at EPS and manage school web applications.

Simultaneously served as Head Developer of EPSchedule, a schedule display website used by the majority of the 600+ person campus.

---

## What Was Done

- Led maintenance and development of EPSchedule: troubleshooting issues, confirming schedule accuracy, adding features (e.g., lunch displays)
- First contribution as a freshman: fixed a bug that broke daily lunch display for 600+ students — praised by teachers and peers
- Grew club membership from 2 to 16
- Planned and launched the first Hackathon in EPS history, coordinating with members, Hack Club org, and school faculty; doubled club membership
- Organized diverse activities: external tech speakers, a new-student orientation game
- Designed a 3D modeling "Jam" tutorial for international Hack Club distribution (paid)
- Interned with the Hack Club organization to develop their website (unpaid)
- Collaborated with the graduating past maintainer to transfer club leadership
